Site redesign

One dark page for eleven papers, a knowledge map and three tools, with four sub-pages reached from it.

A clickable prototype, not the rebuilt site. One long page, four sub-pages, one stylesheet. Everything in this folder can be opened, clicked and argued with.

01

The idea

The site is a single long page: a name, a research list, a map of what you are learning, three tools, and a way to write to you. There is no top navigation. Sub-pages open from the page itself, and each one carries only a way back.

Your old site buried that in weight: nineteen megabyte backgrounds, stencil wordmarks saved as images, every page duplicated in two languages, a navigation that needed a manual, and a blog nobody could find behind a planet.

The cover is a Klein bottle, a surface that cannot be embedded in three dimensions without crossing itself. Its points carry a fourth coordinate, turn in two planes and are projected down to the screen, so the shape keeps folding through itself. It is drawn live, it can be dragged, and it replaces the astronaut photograph and the wordmark image with something that is actually about modelling.

One ground, one accent: near black #0A0B0E, warm paper ink, a single starlight blue. Dark is not a mode here. It is the site.

Open questions

  • 1. 研究条目要显示会议和年份吗？现在只有 001 标了 DEIS 2025，其余只标方向。如果愿意公开投稿去向，每行右侧可以再加一列。
  • 2. 21 张图版怎么做？知识页里 Art 那块是重画的线框树，也可以继续用你原来的思维导图截图，但会跟版式打架。
  • 3. 双语怎么放？现在是两套页面，改动要改两遍；也可以做成同页切换。
  • 5. 封面要不要再大一点？现在它已经溢出到视口右侧，再大就会压到标题，需要你定一个度。
